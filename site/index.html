About Starsand Island

Starsand Island is developed and published by Seed Sparkle Lab, a Chinese studio. The game entered Early Access in February 2026 and reached its full version 1.0 release on August 18, 2026, launching digitally on PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2 on the same day. The standard price on Steam is $39.99. Physical editions for PS5 and Switch 2 are scheduled for November 12, 2026.

The official pitch is simple: "Tired of the city grind? Escape to Starsand Island and embrace the simple joys of country life." In practice that means farming and ranching, fishing, crafting, building and decorating a countryside villa, cruising the coastline by skateboard, scooter, or boat, and forming bonds with the islanders — including finding "that special someone to share your island life with." The game supports four languages — Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese — all with full voice acting, plus single-player and online multiplayer for up to four players, Steam achievements, and full controller support.

On Steam the game currently sits at Very Positive, with roughly 84% of about 3,200 user reviews positive (checked August 25, 2026). If you want the primary sources, start with the official Steam page, join the official Discord, browse the r/Starsandisland subreddit, or watch the 1.0 launch trailer on the official YouTube channel.
