Starsand Island Characters: All NPCs, Romance & Heart Events

Last updated {{UPDATED}} · Based on version 1.0 and community sources — items marked “TBC” are awaiting verification.

Starsand Island has 15 romanceable NPCs, all of whom have official 3-heart and 5-heart story events — and 6 of them have official in-depth date profiles published by the developer (the five profession mentors plus Solara). Below is every character confirmed in official sources: the 6 profiled romance NPCs, the other 9 romanceable NPCs with heart events, and the supporting cast. The developer has not published a complete character encyclopedia, so this list covers what official guides and patch notes confirm; more minor NPCs exist in game.

Friendship Basics: What Each Heart Level Unlocks

Per the official "Getting to Know New Friends" guide, every NPC can be befriended, and each heart level unlocks something concrete:

  • 1❤ — the NPC starts giving you return gifts.
  • 2❤ — you can take photos together.
  • 3❤ — you can enter their room; romanceable NPCs' 3❤ story event and dating unlock around here.
  • 4❤ — you can post Bulletin Board requests for them.
  • 5❤ — you can invite them to move in (see the romance guide).

Each NPC accepts one gift per day, so pick it well — the gift guide has the full loved/liked tables.
