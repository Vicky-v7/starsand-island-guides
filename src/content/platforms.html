Multiplayer: What "Up to 4 Players" Actually Means

Online multiplayer arrived with 1.0 — the official announcement promises you can "farm, fish, build, explore, and enjoy island life together" with up to four players, a figure confirmed identically by the Steam, Nintendo, and PlayStation store pages. Key mechanics from the official pre-launch post:

  • Host-based islands: the host picks Anyone Can Join, Request to Join, or Password Required. Official tip for Switch 2 players: set your island to Password Required to invite friends.
  • Progress is not shared: "Your island progress is not shared in multiplayer" — each player keeps their own island, and some quests are tied to whichever island you're standing on.
  • Permission controls: hosts grant visitors access per category — Crops, Livestock, Fishing, Crafting — and can change it anytime.
  • Multiplayer Coins: a separate currency earned by playing together, spent in a dedicated Multiplayer Shop.
  • No cross-platform play: official statement — "cross-platform multiplayer is not currently supported. We're looking into the possibilities." No date promised.

Be aware of a common complaint in reviews: multiplayer unlocks only after a few hours of single-player story progress, and guests' session gains largely benefit the host's island.

Which Version Should You Buy?

  • Best overall: PC (Steam). Patches land on PC first — the official policy is that console updates follow each platform's certification process — and it's the only platform with modding activity. No Mac/Linux support, though.
  • Console caveat: critic reviews of the Switch 2 version in particular cite heavy bugs and performance issues at launch, so console buyers may want to wait a patch cycle or two.
  • Playing with friends? Everyone needs the same platform (no cross-play), and PS5 players also need PS Plus for online sessions.
  • Xbox streamers: the game isn't in Game Pass; you must buy it, after which Premium/Ultimate tiers can cloud-stream it.
