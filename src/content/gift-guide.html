Starsand Island Gift Guide: Every NPC's Loved & Liked Gifts

Last updated {{UPDATED}} · Based on version 1.0 and community sources — items marked “TBC” are awaiting verification.

Short answer: you can give each NPC one gift per day; a favorite gift is worth roughly +15 affection, a liked gift +10, and a neutral one +5. If you don't know someone's taste yet, Pasture Grass and Celeste Berry are the safe universal picks — nearly everyone at least likes them. The tables below compile the community's most thoroughly verified gift data (the Steam gift guide by Whimsy & Runa, cross-checked against other sources), plus the official heart-event facts.

How Gifting and Affection Work

  • One gift per NPC per day (reported by PCGamesN; single source, so treat as the practical rule (TBC)).
  • Point tiers (reported by TheGamer, partially corroborated by PCGamesN): Favorite +15, Liked +10, Neutral +5, Disliked/Hated 0. Daily chat adds +5; bulletin board requests give +15 to +25; quests up to +50. These exact numbers come from third-party testing, not official docs (TBC).
  • Heart events (official): 15 NPCs have 3❤ and 5❤ story events. The 3❤ event itself rewards 700 Coins + 50 Starsand + a further +60 affection; the 5❤ event rewards 2,000 Coins + 100 Starsand.
  • Most gift data below was collected during Early Access; version 1.0 may have adjusted individual items.

Safe Gifts — and the Moonflower Myth

Two items are near-universal: Pasture Grass (liked by almost everyone; actually loved by Graminova, Marston, Matilda, and Sagewood) and Celeste Berry (liked by almost everyone; loved by Marston).

Myth check: a claim circulating in search results says "Moonflower and Plain Noodles are liked by everyone." The verified community data contradicts it — Loren hates Moonflower (and Red Sleep Lily), Matilda hates Plain Noodles, and Sagewood dislikes them. Moonflower is a great gift for most islanders, but it is not universal. Don't give Loren flowers; give him tech parts and coffee.

Sugar Painting: the +30 Special Gift

Once you reach 2 hearts with an NPC, you can take a photo together, then bring the photo to the stall lady near Soluna Cafe and pay 100 coins for a custom Sugar Painting of that character (one per day; if she says "this one is a bit hard to draw," retake the photo). Given to the right character, it's worth up to +30 affection — but reactions vary, and testers documented an apparent bug where some characters say "Adored" yet grant no points (observed pre-1.0; current status (TBC)). Verified best responder: Aurelis (+30). Loren gives +10; Neona, Serena, and Stella +5; Alex is neutral.

What We Deliberately Left Out

To keep this page trustworthy: the rumored birthday 3x bonus has no surviving source (the one article claiming it is dead) and no confirmed heart-point thresholds exist in any first-hand source, so neither appears here. Where the community sources conflict (e.g., one site claims Zerine loves Copper Sheet; testers found she dislikes it), we follow the tested data.
